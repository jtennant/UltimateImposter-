How to play

The idea

Everyone gets the same secret word — except the imposter, who's flying blind and has to fake it. Crew are hunting the faker; the imposter is hunting the word.

A round

  1. Deal. Pass the phone round. Each player holds to see their role in private, then passes on.
  2. Clues. In the order shown, everyone says one word about the secret word. No sentences, no spelling it, no rhymes, no "it's the thing we saw last week".
  3. Discuss. Argue about whose clue was too vague, too safe, or suspiciously perfect.
  4. Vote. On three, everyone points. Tap whoever got the most votes.
  5. Last chance. If the imposter is caught, they get one guess at the word. Nail it and they steal the win.

Winning

  • Crew win if they vote out every imposter and the imposter can't guess the word.
  • Imposter wins by surviving the vote, or by guessing the secret word after being caught.

Modes

  • Classic. Imposter is told they're the imposter and sees the category as a hint.
  • Blackout. Imposter gets nothing at all. Brutal.
  • Undercover. Everyone gets a word, but the imposter's word is subtly different — and nobody is told who's who. You have to work out whether the odd one out is you.

Good clue, bad clue

Word is Pizza. Good: "Naples", "delivery", "slice". Too safe: "food" — it tells the crew nothing and screams imposter. Too good: "pepperoni" — you've just handed the imposter the word.

How many imposters

  • 3–5 players: 1
  • 6–8 players: 1–2
  • 9+ players: 2–3
